# Amira AI — the world's first AI-native Customer Operations Platform

**Close the case, not the conversation.**

Amira takes ownership of every customer case from first contact to final resolution. It connects to the systems an organisation already runs (CRM, ERP, telephony, HR), acts inside them, escalates by SLA, and hands over to a human with a full summary whenever needed. Every interaction is analysed for intent, NPS, CSAT and process gaps, so the AI and the human team keep improving together.

- **100% hallucination-free** answers, grounded in the customer's own systems
- **Any channel:** phone, WhatsApp, chat, email, and video avatars that navigate websites and kiosks
- **120 languages**, including Gulf and Saudi Arabic
- **Built and run in Dubai**

**Customers include** Danube Properties and Volkswagen Group Retail.
**Award:** Best AI for Customer Engagement, CX Middle East.
